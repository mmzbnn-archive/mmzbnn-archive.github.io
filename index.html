About

I am a researcher interested in language & A.I., currently pursuing a PhD in responsible AI at Utrecht University, with previous work on robustness testing, human alignment, and various language phenomena (e.g. humour and insults). I am broadly interested in how we create, relate to, and embody technologies; the risks they entail, the ways they shape memory and emotion; and language as a technology. I have recently started to work on the projects listed here, and I am open to collaborations concerning language, technology and AI.
